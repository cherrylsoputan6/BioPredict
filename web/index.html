Feature values and provenance


How to read a variant
R175H means arginine (R) at protein position 175 changes to histidine (H). Positions start at 1 in the selected MANE protein; another transcript may use different numbering.
The model excludes start-codon changes, stop changes, insertions/deletions, unchanged residues and noncanonical amino acids. Unknown genes, reference mismatches or fewer than three qualifying species produce an unavailable result—not a benign prediction.
What the features describe
Relative position locates the residue in the protein. Signed and absolute hydrophobicity/weight changes describe the substitution. BLOSUM62 describes general substitution patterns. Ortholog agreement counts how often the reference and alternate appear at the aligned site in five species; it does not by itself establish pathogenicity.


Privacy and availability
Use public research examples; do not submit patient identifiers or records. Gene/transcript lookups are sent to NCBI and Ensembl. Prediction results are temporary server memory; anyone with a result link can retrieve it until it expires. Reports download to your device.
A restart can erase jobs and cached downloads. The research service may refuse requests while busy or when providers are unavailable. Hosting and upstream services may retain request logs.


Evidence and limitations
The baseline is frozen. Sequence-context features remain experimental and are not used here.
Earlier evaluation achieved 81.8% accuracy on 176 covered test variants, including 27 benign variants incorrectly flagged. That split has already been evaluated and is not an untouched final holdout.
Corrected scientific validation groups gene aliases and identical proteins together. Ortholog coverage differs by class; scores do not establish performance on new protein families or clinical populations.
Unsupported changes and missing alignment coverage produce an unavailable result. Requests use NCBI and the October 2024 Ensembl archive; no results are published.
