Evidence and limitations
The baseline is frozen. Sequence-context features remain experimental and are not used here.
Earlier evaluation achieved 81.8% accuracy on 176 covered test variants, including 27 benign variants incorrectly flagged. That split has already been evaluated and is not an untouched final holdout.
Corrected scientific validation groups gene aliases and identical proteins together. Ortholog coverage differs by class; scores do not establish performance on new protein families or clinical populations.
Unsupported changes and missing alignment coverage produce an unavailable result. Requests use NCBI and the October 2024 Ensembl archive; no results are published.
